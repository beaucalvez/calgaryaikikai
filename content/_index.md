Since 1980, Calgary Aikikai has trained hundreds of students in this martial art that is both a practical self-defence and form of creative movement.

## Adult Aikido Program

Our adult program is open to all ages, genders, and abilities. Classes focus on proper technique and mental focus as well as overall fitness and flexibility. We offer **4 free trial classes** so you can experience aikido before committing.

## Kids' Aikido Program

Our children's program is for ages 6–12, with youth classes for ages 11–13. Kids develop good discipline and a healthy attitude while enjoying a fun and rigorous practice. Teens (13+) may join adult classes based on maturity.

**4 free trial classes available.**
