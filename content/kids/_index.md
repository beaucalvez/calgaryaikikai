Calgary Aikikai's children's programme offers aikido instruction for kids in a safe, encouraging, and fun environment.

## Age Groups

| Group | Ages |
|-------|------|
| **Children** | Ages 6–10 |
| **Youth** | Ages 11–13 |
| **Teens** | 13+ may join adult classes based on maturity |

---

## Class Schedule

| Day | Time | Class |
|-----|------|-------|
| Wednesday | 6:30–7:30 PM | Combined Children & Youth |
| Saturday | 10:00–11:00 AM | Children (Ages 6–10) |
| Saturday | 10:15–11:15 AM | Youth (Ages 11–13) |

**Note:** The regular schedule runs from the first Saturday after Labour Day to the last Saturday before Canada Day. Closed Christmas and New Year's. On long weekends, youth join adults 10:15–11:15 AM; no children's classes.

---

## Benefits of Aikido for Kids

- **Self-confidence and calm disposition** — Aikido's non-competitive nature builds inner confidence
- **Focus and attention to detail** — Learning complex movements requires and develops concentration
- **Body awareness, flexibility, and fitness** — Physical conditioning through safe, fun movement
- **Cooperation, compassion, and leadership** — Training with partners of all levels builds empathy and teamwork
