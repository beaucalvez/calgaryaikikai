## Six-Week Aikido Intro Course

New to aikido? Our six-week introductory course is the perfect starting point. No prior experience is necessary — just a willingness to learn.

**Requirements:** Must be 16 years or older.

**Cost:** $95 for the full six-week course.

**Class limit:** 16 students per session.

---

## What You'll Learn (9 hours total)

Over six weeks, you'll cover:

- Dojo etiquette, greetings, and bowing
- Basic body movement and footwork techniques
- Joint locks, immobilizations, and throws
- Ukemi — safe falling and receiving of techniques
- Weapons: staff (jo), wooden sword (bokken), wooden knife (tanto)
- Defence against strikes, grips, and multiple attackers

The philosophy underlying all of this: blending with an attacker's motion to redirect and resolve aggression — the "Art of Peace."
