A reference for Japanese terms used in aikido training.

## Greetings & Phrases

| Japanese | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **Onegai shimasu** | "Please teach me" — said while bowing at the start of practice |
| **Domo arigato gozaimashita** | "Thank you very much" — said while bowing at the end of practice |
| **Sumimasen** | Excuse me / I'm sorry |
| **Hai** | Yes |
| **Ohayo gozaimasu** | Good morning |
| **Konnichiwa** | Hello (daytime) |
| **Oyasumi nasai** | Goodnight |
| **Rei** | Bow |

## Basic Terms

| Japanese | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **Aikido** | "The way of harmonizing spirit" |
| **Sensei** | Teacher |
| **Dojo** | "Place of the way" — training hall |
| **Kamae** | Posture, stance |
| **Maai** | Distance between two persons |
| **Tachi waza** | Standing techniques |
| **Uke** | The person who receives the nage's movement |
| **Nage** | The person who performs the movement |
| **Ukemi** | Falls or receiving of throws |

## Body Movements (Taisabaki)

| Japanese | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **Irimi** | Entering |
| **Tenkan** | Turning |
| **Kaiten** | Enter and turn |
| **Omote waza** | Moving in front of |
| **Ura waza** | Moving behind |

## Techniques (Defenses)

| Japanese | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **Ikkyo** | First principle — arm immobilization |
| **Nikyo** | Second principle — wrist bending |
| **Sankyo** | Third principle — hand and wrist twisting |
| **Iriminage** | Body entering throw |
| **Kokyunage** | Breath throw |

## Attacks

| Japanese | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **Shomenuchi** | Strike to the front of the head |
| **Yokomenuchi** | Strike to the side of the head |
| **Tsuki** | Punch or thrust |
| **Kubishime** | Choking, strangling |
| **Ryotedori** | Holding both wrists from the front |
