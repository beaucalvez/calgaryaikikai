Testing at Calgary Aikikai follows a structured credit system to ensure students are well-prepared before advancing in rank.

## Credit System

- **1 credit** is earned per class attended
- Seminar credits are determined at the time of each event
- Credits are counted from the date of your previous test

## Testing Schedule

Testing occurs approximately every **3–4 months**. Your instructor will let you know when you are ready to test.

## Rank Structure

**Kyu Levels** — Pre-black belt ranks (6th kyu through 1st kyu)

**Yudansha Levels** — Black belt ranks (Shodan / 1st Dan and above)
