Calgary Aikikai offers flexible enrollment options for Kids, Youths, and Adults. All memberships include black belt instruction, a uniform, and your first belt test (where applicable).

For current pricing and to register, please visit our online store:

**[store.calgaryaikikai.com](https://store.calgaryaikikai.com)**

---

## Kids (Ages 6–12)

- Up to 2 classes weekly (Wednesday evenings & Saturday mornings)
- Enrollment options: 3-month intro, monthly, sessional, or yearly
- Includes: uniform, black belt instruction

## Youths (Ages 13–17)

- Up to 3 classes per week
- Flexible enrollment options
- Includes: uniform, crest, first belt test, black belt instruction

## Adults (Ages 18+)

- Up to 3 classes weekly
- Flexible enrollment options
- Includes: uniform, crest, first belt test, black belt instruction
