We are thrilled to share some exciting news from our dojo community!

**Chris Hayward** was recently presented with his **6th Dan promotion certificate**. Chris has been training with Calgary Aikikai since 1996 under Inaba Shihan and served as Dojo-cho for 20 years. This promotion is a recognition of his decades of dedication, skilled teaching, and contributions to our community. Congratulations, Chris Sensei!

**Andrew Barron** received his **5th Dan certificate**. Andrew achieved shodan (black belt) in 2006 and has been a committed and skilled practitioner ever since. This promotion reflects his consistent practice and growth over the years. Congratulations, Andrew Sensei!

Both promotions reflect the high standards and deep commitment to training that define Calgary Aikikai. We are proud of both of them and grateful for everything they contribute to our dojo.

Onwards and upwards!
