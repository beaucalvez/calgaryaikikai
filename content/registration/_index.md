## How to Register

New students receive **4 free trial classes** before committing to a membership. After your trial classes, registration is completed online.

### Step 1: Mailing List

Sign up for our mailing list to stay informed about schedule changes, seminars, and dojo news:

**[Join our mailing list](https://calgaryaikikai.us1.list-manage.com/subscribe?u=7fc806892a212f70339c37d51&id=fd658e80d9)**

### Step 2: Online Store / Membership

To complete your membership registration and pay fees, visit our Square store:

**[store.calgaryaikikai.com](https://store.calgaryaikikai.com)**
