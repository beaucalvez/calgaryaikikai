Aikido is a modern Japanese martial art founded by Morihei Ueshiba in the early 20th century. Unlike martial arts that block or meet force with force, aikido emphasizes blending with an attacker's motion and redirecting their energy in circular movements.

## Philosophy

Training aikido requires a calm, relaxed mind and body and a great deal of self-control. Central to practice is the concept of **Ki** — vital energy — which is cultivated through warm-up exercises and refined through technique practice.

Aikido is non-competitive, creating an enjoyable and cooperative learning environment. It is movement-based rather than strength-dependent, making it accessible to all ages and abilities.

## What You'll Learn

Training follows a natural progression:

1. **Ukemi** — Safe falling and receiving techniques
2. **Basic movements** — Footwork (taisabaki), entering (irimi), turning (tenkan)
3. **Techniques** — Joint locks, immobilizations, and throws that work with natural joint ranges of motion
4. **Weapons** — Wooden sword (bokken), staff (jo), and knife (tanto)
5. **Advanced perception** — Intuition, timing, and blending developed over years of practice

Techniques conclude with throws or joint immobilizations that resolve aggression and create harmony — what founder Ueshiba called the **"Art of Peace."**
