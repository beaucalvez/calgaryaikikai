We were thrilled to welcome **Cyndy Hayashi Shihan (7th Dan)** to Calgary for a three-day spring seminar.

## About Cyndy Hayashi Shihan

Cyndy Hayashi Shihan is the Chief Instructor of Aikido West in California, with over 45 years of training experience. She is known for her engaging teaching style and spirited, enjoyable approach to aikido that inspires students of all levels.
